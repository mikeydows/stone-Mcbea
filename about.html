About Us

Welcome to Stone & Mcbea Creatives, a creative graphic design partnership proudly based in Accra, Ghana.

Our Story

was born from a shared passion for creativity and visual storytelling. As two designers with different strengths but one vision, we decided to come together to build something bigger than ourselves. What started as a small idea between partners has grown into a full creative studio dedicated to helping brands look professional, modern, and unforgettable.

What we are

We are a partnership of two professional graphic designers who believe that great design is not just about looking good, but about communicating the right message. We combine creativity, strategy, and experience to deliver designs that connect with your audience.

What We Do

We offer a complete range of graphic design services for businesses, startups, churches, schools, and individuals. Our services include:

  • Logo Design & Brand Identity
  • Flyers, Posters & Banners
  • Birthday & Event Designs
  • Business Cards, Letterheads & Company Profiles
  • Social Media Designs & Advertising Graphics
  • Packaging & Label Design
  • T-Shirt & Souvenir Branding
  • Website Design/Developing
  • Photography/Videography
  • Video editing & Picture retouching
Whether you need a simple flyer or a full brand identity, we have you covered.

Why Choose Us?

  • Two Minds, One Vision: You get the creativity and ideas of two designers on every project.
  • Creative & Modern: We don't do boring designs. Our work is clean, modern, and eye-catching.
  • Fast & Reliable: We respect your time and deliver on time.
  • Affordable: Professional designs at prices that small businesses in Ghana can afford.
  • Client-Focused: We listen to your ideas and bring them to life exactly how you want it.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help Ghanaian businesses and brands grow through powerful, professional, and affordable graphic design.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted and creative design partnerships in Ghana and beyond.
